## Glossary
- x:anti_mainstream_medicine_framing: Conventional medicine or ordinary doctors are portrayed as ignorant, captured, or unable to help. Discussing the health system as a subject is the Health-Care System topic; this label is for the rhetoric.
- x:big_food_food_system_distrust: The food industry is cast as deliberately or negligently harming health. An allegation that the food supply is intentionally poisoned goes to Food conspiracy.
- x:biohacking_framing: Self-experimentation framing specifically: n-of-1 trials, stacks, protocols, quantified routines.
- x:commercialization: The passage sells or monetises: sponsorship, discount codes, affiliate links, or the speaker’s own product, clinic, programme or book. Applies to a host’s own products as well as paid reads; set relevance to advertisement only inside a delimited ad break.
- x:conspiracy_cover_up: A coordinated, hidden and malign effort is alleged. Requires an actual allegation of coordination or concealment; criticism, distrust or incompetence alone is not enough.
- x:credential_appeal: A person’s title, training or professional identity is invoked as grounds for believing a claim.
- x:evidence_strength_claim: An explicit assertion about how well established something is: proven, clinically proven, science-backed, evidence-based, FDA approved.
- x:government_institution_distrust: Public agencies, regulators or official science are portrayed as corrupt, lying or not to be trusted.
- x:maha_adjacent_online_vocabulary: The online shorthand cluster that signals alignment without the movement’s formal vocabulary: seed oils, fluoride, medical freedom, vaccine injury, corporate capture.
- x:mechanistic_scientific_language: Biological mechanism vocabulary is used to explain a claim or make it sound plausible, without a study being cited.
- x:medical_freedom: Health decisions are framed in terms of autonomy, informed consent, parental rights or freedom from mandate.
- x:misinformation_correction_debunking: The speaker corrects, fact-checks or debunks a health claim. Apply regardless of whether the correction is itself accurate.
- x:naturalness_appeal: Something is recommended or condemned on the grounds of being natural, ancestral, traditional, clean or chemical-free.
- x:optimization_framing: Health is framed as a performance problem to be maximised, with protocols, stacks, targets and peak states.
- x:personal_experience_evidence: Personal or second-hand experience is offered as grounds for a general conclusion. Apply only where experience functions as evidence, not to every anecdote.
- x:prestige_science_invocation: A prestigious institution, journal or affiliation is named to lend authority, rather than a specific finding being discussed on its content.
- x:purity_contamination_framing: Moral or aesthetic clean-versus-dirty, pure-versus-poisoned language applied to foods, products or bodies.
- x:root_cause_framing: Getting to an underlying cause is contrasted with merely treating symptoms, or reversal is promised in place of management.
- x:scientific_study_citation: A study, trial or research literature is invoked as support for a point. Applies whether or not the study is named, real or correctly described.
- x:toxin_framing: Health problems are attributed to accumulated toxins or chemical burden in general terms. A named exposure discussed on its evidence is the Environmental Health topic.
- x:weak_evidence_extrapolation_signal: The evidence actually offered is preliminary, indirect or anecdotal — animal or cell studies, observational association, preprints, single cases. Code the evidence the speaker offers, not your judgement of whether their conclusion is warranted.
- t:cardiometabolic_health: Cardiovascular and metabolic disease and its clinical markers and drugs: blood pressure, lipids, ApoB, statins, diabetes, fatty liver. Weight and dieting as such go to Weight Loss & Metabolic Health.
- t:childrens_health: Health of infants, children and adolescents, and the products, medications and exposures specific to them. Autism causation goes to Neurodevelopment / Autism Discourse; the childhood vaccine schedule to Vaccines & Immunization.
- t:covid_19_pandemic_health: SARS-CoV-2 infection, long COVID, pandemic-era measures and policies, and COVID treatments. COVID vaccines belong to Vaccines & Immunization unless the subject is the disease, the pandemic response, or its origin.
- t:dementia_cognitive_decline_ageing: Age-related cognitive decline and the conditions and care around it. Longevity and anti-ageing protocols go to Longevity / Anti-Ageing.
- t:diet_tribes_food_ideologies: Named dietary patterns and the identities and arguments around them. Discussion of a single food or nutrient on its merits goes to Food & Nutrition.
- t:emf_technology_exposure: Health effects attributed to electromagnetic fields, wireless technology, and screen or blue-light exposure.
- t:fitness_exercise: Exercise itself: modality, dose, programming, technique and fitness metrics. Post-exercise recovery modalities go to Recovery & Physical Optimization.
- t:fluoride_oral_health: Teeth, gums, the oral microbiome, and fluoride in water and dental products.
- t:food_nutrition: Foods, nutrients, additives and the health effects attributed to them. A named dietary pattern or identity goes to Diet Tribes; pills, powders and nutraceuticals to Functional Nutrition & Supplements.
- t:functional_nutrition_supplements: Supplements, powders and nutraceuticals: what is in them, what they are claimed to do, and how they are sold. Nutrients as eaten in food go to Food & Nutrition.
- t:genetics_inheritance: Heredity and genetic explanation: what is inherited, how traits and blood types pass on, genetic testing and conditions with a genetic cause.
- t:health_care_system_medical_trust: Medicine’s institutions, costs, incentives, regulators and trustworthiness discussed as a subject. Distrust expressed as rhetoric rather than discussed as a subject is coded on the frame axis.
- t:hearing_vision_sensory_health: The senses and their loss, correction and aids.
- t:longevity_anti_aging: Lifespan and healthspan extension: biological-age testing, longevity drugs, clinics and protocols aimed explicitly at ageing. General self-optimisation with no longevity goal goes to Biohacking / Human Optimization.
- t:mental_health: Psychiatric conditions, their diagnosis and treatment, therapy, and popular psychology vocabulary. Cortisol and nervous-system framing goes to Stress / Nervous-System Regulation.
- t:natural_alternative_functional_medicine: Alternative, integrative and complementary systems of care and their practitioners. Apply when a modality or practice is actually discussed; the anti-mainstream rhetoric that often accompanies it is a separate frame label.
- t:neurodevelopment_autism_discourse: Autism and neurodevelopmental conditions: causes, prevalence, treatment and cure claims, and the debate around them.
- t:other_health_topic: Substantive health, medicine or wellness content that no other topic label fits. Name the subject in the summary. Never use it as a second-choice label when a listed topic applies — it exists to make gaps in the taxonomy visible.
- t:pain_musculoskeletal_health: Musculoskeletal pain and injury and their treatment, including manual therapy and rehabilitation.
- t:peptides_experimental_optimization_compounds: Research peptides and growth-hormone secretagogues used for recovery, fat loss, cognition or longevity. Anabolic steroids and SARMs go to Performance-Enhancing Drugs; incretin peptides to GLP-1 / Incretin Drugs.
- t:pregnancy_maternal_postpartum_health: Conception onward: pregnancy, birth, the postpartum period, infant feeding, and medications or exposures during pregnancy. Fertility treatment and abortion go to Reproductive & Sexual Health.
- t:public_health_policy: Government and institutional health policy: mandates, regulation, guidelines, funding and their politics.
- t:recovery_physical_optimization: Modalities used to recover or feel better physically — heat, cold, compression, light, bodywork, mobility work. The systematic-optimisation framing around them is coded on the frame axis, not here.
- t:regenerative_aesthetic_medicine: Platelet, stem-cell, exosome and related regenerative injections for aesthetic or orthopaedic use. Topical skincare and standard dermatology go to Beauty / Dermatology / Skincare.
- t:sleep: Sleep quality, duration, timing and disorders, and the practices and products sold to improve them. Fatigue attributed to a named chronic illness goes to Chronic / Complex Symptom Communities.
- t:substance_use_addiction_harm_reduction: Dependence, overdose, treatment, recovery and harm reduction for drugs of misuse. Cannabis and psychedelics, nicotine and caffeine, and alcohol each have their own topic.
- t:vaccines_immunization: Vaccines themselves: specific vaccines, the childhood schedule, mandates and exemptions, efficacy, ingredients, and alleged adverse effects. Apply to vaccine content whatever the stance; conspiracy or distrust framing is coded separately on the frame axis.
- t:wearables_self_tracking_digital_health: Devices, at-home tests and apps used to measure the body, and the interpretation of what they report.
- t:weight_loss_metabolic_health: Body weight, dieting for weight loss, obesity, and metabolic dysfunction discussed as a weight or lifestyle matter. Clinical cardiovascular and diabetes markers and their drugs go to Cardiometabolic Health.
- t:womens_health_female_hormones: Female reproductive endocrinology and conditions across the life course, including menopause and hormone therapy. Pregnancy and postpartum go to Pregnancy / Maternal / Postpartum Health; contraception, abortion and fertility treatment to Reproductive & Sexual Health.
