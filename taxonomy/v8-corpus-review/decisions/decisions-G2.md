## Proposals

- ACCEPT  topic:sleep.other_sleep_disorders  About 1,707 episodes on 189 podcasts, roughly half of them literal. Neither insomnia nor apnea fits. - ACCEPT  topic:immune.inflammation  Oxidative stress, free radicals and ROS appear in 2,032 episodes on 79 podcasts with no home, and nearly every hit co-occurs with inflammation. - MODIFY  topic:policy.partisan_politics  The definition gets one clause on fights over inclusive medical language. The examples now use real sample phrasing ("vulva owners"). I dropped "chestfeeding", which no sample showed.
- ACCEPT  topic:gut.digestive_symptoms  Food sensitivities: 1,235 episodes on 129 podcasts, with a three-way split seen in the blind sample. The row adds non-allergic sensitivities and boundaries to `immune.allergies`, `diets.elimination_therapeutic` and `self_tracking.consumer_lab_tests`.

## Codebook

Report 15's codebook and prompt suggestions that I judge sound:

1. **Section 5.1, "General talk takes the parent": mental health.** "'Mental health' in general, undiagnosed 'mental health issues', mental-health awareness or stigma, and a person's or society's mental health declining with no condition named are `topic:mental`; `mental.wellbeing_grief_loneliness` is for mood, happiness, loneliness, grief and burnout as emotion." Also: speculation that a real person has undiagnosed mental illness is a `passing` `topic:mental` detection with no claim. The idioms "mental health day" and "mental health moment" are excluded. Evidence: 23,849 episodes on 391 podcasts; in a 16-hit sample, 5 of 13 health hits had no tie-break (4086/2, 9728/218, 22589/9, 175685/2, 161451/6). This is the largest-volume fix in the report.
2. **Section 3, exclusion for sports availability.** "Healthy", "health" or "health issues" meaning an athlete is available to play, with no injury, condition or treatment named, is not health content; a named injury is `musculoskeletal.sports_injuries` (`passing`). Evidence: 3,683 episodes on 199 podcasts (Simmons 469, Fantasy Footballers 467, PMT 323); 165304/3, 79799/1, 163577/4.
3. **Section 3, boundary for self-help and relationship psychology.** Personal-development talk (mindset, self-talk, shame, "healing" in a moral or relational sense, "train your nervous system" as a metaphor for courage) is health content only when it names a mental-health condition, symptom, treatment or a practice offered for health; otherwise exclude it. Evidence: the self-help batch was 50% boundary-unclear (188022/2, 342/4, 187636/1, 187472/0), and the shows are large (SoG 1,995, Mindset Mentor 1,927 and Shetty 883 listed episodes). This fits the new `narrative:mind_cures_disease` and `narrative:trauma_stored_in_body`: a "healing" claim about physical disease is health content.
4. **Section 3, rule for delivered guided practice.** A guided meditation, breathing exercise, sleep story or hypnosis script delivered to the listener takes one `passing` detection of its practice per window (`stress.meditation_mindfulness`, `stress.breathwork`, `sleep.sleep_hygiene_environment`). The scripted instructions are not claims; stated health effects are coded normally. Evidence: Meditation for Anxiety 1,482, Radio Headspace 1,751, Get Sleepy 787, Nothing Much Happens 510 and Sleep Magic 271 listed episodes; 203253/1.
5. **Section 3, death with no cause.** Extend the violence and death paragraph beyond crime: a death named with no cause, illness, injury or care ("my dad passed away last May") is not health content. Grief over it is `mental.wellbeing_grief_loneliness` only when the grief is discussed. Evidence: 58451/8, 6595/7; the family-illness batch.
6. **Section 3, ads that list insurance products.** An insurance or financial ad that lists health insurance among other products fails test (a) unless health coverage is the featured product. Evidence: Ramsey's insurance-hub read (57986/9, 57596/10). This is consistent with report 09's note that car and life insurance ads add noise to `costs_insurance`.
7. **Section 5.1, rules 3 and 6: "healthcare" as a political list item.** "Healthcare" named as one issue among non-health issues takes no detection. A stated position or claim about coverage or cost takes `health_system.costs_insurance`, plus `policy.partisan_politics` when party positions or campaigns are themselves discussed. Evidence: 578001/0, 165440/2, 330395/137.
8. **Section 5.1, "Unsettled facts", tumours (modified).** Merge this into the existing paragraph rather than adding a rule. A "tumor" with no cancer word or type takes the organ's cancer subtopic (`cancer.other_specific_cancers` for brain tumours, which already lists "brain tumor") at confidence 0.6 or less, the existing threshold. A tumour stated to be benign (meningioma, fibroid, lipoma) takes the organ system's subtopic. Evidence: "brain tumor" in 827 episodes on 163 podcasts; 74507/4.
9. **Section 5.1, caregiving.** Caring for a family member with a named illness takes that illness's subtopic, plus `stress.stress_burnout` or `mental.wellbeing_grief_loneliness` for the carer's strain. `dementia_ageing.elder_care` applies only when the person cared for is old or frail. Evidence: 178026/0, 179137/5; 2,543 "caregiv*" episodes, many of them meaning childcare.
10. **Section 5.1, rule 8: prescription drugs used non-medically.** Recreational prescription opioids are `drugs_addiction.opioids_fentanyl`. Benzodiazepines and sleeping pills are `mental.psychiatric_drugs` or `sleep.insomnia`, plus `drugs_addiction.addiction_recovery` when addiction or treatment is discussed. Evidence: 323909/7, 163340/4; Xanax-family terms in 2,490 episodes on 207 podcasts.
11. **Section 5.1, worked tie-breaks (short notes):**
    - Possession versus mental illness: code the mental subtopics plus `frame:spiritual_religious` (205415/1).
    - Somatic-memory talk: a physical symptom attributed to emotion is `stress.mind_body`, and somatic therapy is `stress.nervous_system_regulation`. Add `narrative:trauma_stored_in_body` only when storage or release of trauma is claimed (25951/50).
    - Endocannabinoid-system talk: `psychoactives.cannabis` when CBD or cannabis products are the subject, otherwise `cognition.neurochemistry_talk` (23899/4).
12. **Rubric (`analysis/prompts/rubric-v8.md`), step 1.** Add a short boundary checklist for the high-volume patterns that are not health content: sports availability, self-help metaphor, a bare death, and idioms built on medical words ("sick man of Europe", "sleepwalking through", "mental health day"). Evidence: 44 of 204 blind hits (22%) were not health content, and these patterns made up most of the borderline ones.
